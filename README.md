# Trackify — Expense Tracker
Trackify helps you stay on top of your money by logging what you spend and when you spend it. Whether you’re planning a monthly budget or monitoring day‑to‑day costs, Trackify keeps your spending organized and turns it into clear, useful insights.



## Features

- **Quick expense entry:** Add transactions with date, type/category, and amount in seconds.
- **Visual insights:** Interactive charts highlight trends and patterns over time.
- **Date filtering:** View spending for specific days or custom ranges with ease.
- **Clean, focused UI:** A simple interface that prioritizes clarity over clutter.
